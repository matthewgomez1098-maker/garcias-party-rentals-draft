FAQs

The questions you answer on the phone twenty times a week.

Every one of these belongs on the website instead, so you stop repeating yourself.

How far ahead should I book?

DRAFT - your real answer here. For summer water slides and quinceañera weekends the honest answer is usually several weeks, and saying so sets expectations before someone rings you the day before.

Do you deliver and set up?

DRAFT - confirm. If you set up and tear down, say it loudly: it is the single biggest reason somebody picks a rental company over collecting chairs themselves.

What do you need at the property?

DRAFT - for jumpers and water slides you need a flat area, access to power within a certain distance, and a hose for water units. Putting the actual numbers here saves you a wasted trip.

Can you set up on concrete or turf?

DRAFT - confirm. Lots of Inland Empire backyards are concrete or artificial turf and customers genuinely do not know whether that rules them out. Answering it wins jobs.

What is your deposit and cancellation policy?

DRAFT - decide and publish it. It is the question that causes every argument, and having it written down in advance is how you avoid them.

Are you insured?

DRAFT - important for inflatables specifically. Many parks and venues will not let a jumper on site without a certificate of insurance. If you carry it, that is a selling point; if a customer needs a COI naming the venue, say whether you can provide one.

Do you speak Spanish?

Si. Your Facebook page is written in Spanish already, so this is just saying out loud what is already true. It belongs on the homepage, not buried in an FAQ.

Tell us the date and how many people.
